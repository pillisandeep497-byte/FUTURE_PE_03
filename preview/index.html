Why this shape

The gap: nobody in the city publishes prices or admits a limitation.

1. Prices in text

"Keratin cost in Visakhapatnam" is the last question before someone books. Three competing salons leave it blank. Publishing a real range with the variables that change it wins the query and pre-qualifies the customer.

2. An honest limitation

Every article says who it isn't for: "if your hair has been bleached twice in three months, this isn't right for you — here's what to do instead." A studio that refuses bookings is more believable than one that takes every one.

3. Two articles a month, not twenty

Google's March 2026 update made scaled content abuse its primary enforcement target — 50–80% traffic drops for sites hit. The cluster is sized to what the owner can personally review.
