## What changes the price

| Factor | Why it moves the price | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| **Hair length** | More product, more sections, more time | +₹1,000 to +₹2,500 |
| **Hair thickness** | Denser hair takes longer to saturate and seal | +₹500 to +₹1,500 |
| **Product range** | Professional brands cost the salon more | +₹1,000 to +₹2,000 |
| **Hair condition** | Damaged hair may need repair first — a separate service | +₹1,500 to +₹3,000 |
| **Service type** | Express/mini keratin (4–6 weeks) vs full keratin (3–5 months) | Express is 40–60% cheaper |
| **Add-ons** | Trim, colour, spa — only if you ask for them | Varies |

**The honest summary:** length and thickness are what most affect your price. If two studios quote very differently for the same hair, ask what treatment type and which products each is using. That usually explains the whole gap.

## What the ₹2,000 keratin actually costs you

A very low quote in Vizag usually means one of four things. None of them is automatically dishonest — but you should know which you're getting.

| What's cut | What it looks like | What it costs you |
|---|---|---|
| **Express treatment sold as full keratin** | Done in 60–90 minutes; fades in 4–6 weeks | You pay again in two months — ₹4,000+ over the year |
| **Entry-level or diluted product** | Works initially, weaker hold | Result fades fast; sometimes a rough texture as it goes |
| **Skipped clarifying wash** | They start applying immediately | Formula can't bond — fails in 3 weeks, hair feels coated |
| **Skipped or rushed sealing** | Blow-dry and go, no sectioned ironing | The smoothness never develops properly |
| **A trainee doing the work** | Ask who is doing your hair | Inconsistent results; little recourse |

The rule of thumb: **ask what's included and how long it takes.** A full keratin takes 3–4 hours. If the answer is "about an hour", you're buying an express treatment, and you should compare it to express pricing, not to keratin.

## Hidden charges worth asking about before you book

No professional studio should surprise you at the counter. Ask these six questions on the phone before you go in:

1. **Is the price for a full keratin or an express treatment?**
2. **Is there an extra charge for hair length or thickness, and roughly how much?**
3. **Does the price include the wash, the trim of the ends, and the finish?**
4. **Will the same stylist who quotes me do the service?**
5. **If my hair needs a repair treatment first, what does that add?**
6. **Is the final price confirmed before you start?**

If a studio can't answer those six, that's your answer. At Glow Studio, the final price is written down before the first section is treated, and it doesn't move afterwards.
