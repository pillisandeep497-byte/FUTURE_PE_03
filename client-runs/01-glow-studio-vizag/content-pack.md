## 1. The strategy in five lines

1. **Your customers search in two moods.** When they're deciding: *"keratin treatment in Visakhapatnam"*, *"how much does it cost"*. When they're doubting: *"does keratin damage hair"*, *"how long does it last"*. No salon in your area answers either one properly.
2. **One pillar, five supports.** The pillar covers keratin completely. Each support owns one question and hands the reader to the pillar.
3. **Prices in text, not in images.** This is the biggest gap in your competitors' websites, and the highest-intent search in the cluster.
4. **Honesty as the differentiator.** Publishing that you refuse bookings when hair isn't suitable is the single strongest trust signal in your category — and no competitor can copy it without actually doing it.
5. **Two articles a month.** Six articles published carefully over three months beat twenty published in a week. Publishing faster than you can review it is exactly what Google's 2026 enforcement targets.
