## Anchor text rules applied

1. **Never "click here", "read more", or "this article".** Every anchor describes the destination.
2. **Vary anchors** — the pillar is linked with four different phrases across the cluster (`Read the full keratin guide`, `full keratin guide`, `Read the full keratin guide for Visakhapatnam`, `See keratin prices in Visakhapatnam`).
3. **No exact-match repetition to the same page** from the same article.
4. **Descriptive > keyword-stuffed.** Only the pillar→cost link uses a near-exact keyword, because the destination genuinely is about that phrase.
5. **Link within body copy, not in sidebars or footers**, so the links carry contextual weight.

## Post-publish link maintenance (monthly, 10 minutes)

1. **Search Console → Links → Internal links.** Check the pillar is receiving the most internal links of any blog page.
2. **Add links to new articles from older ones.** When S5 is published, go back and add a link from the pillar and from S4.
3. **Fix any orphan** — any article with fewer than 2 inbound internal links.
4. **Check for cannibalisation** — if two pages alternate positions for the same query, consolidate: merge the weaker into the stronger and 301 redirect.
5. **Update the pillar quarterly** with a new FAQ or refreshed price range, and change the "Last updated" date only when content genuinely changes.
