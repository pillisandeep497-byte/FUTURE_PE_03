### Deductions explained (not hidden)

- **S2 (42), S3 (41) — check 10, real numbers:** these are informational articles; the numbers they need (product brand names in aftercare, studio's post-treatment interval) are marked `[NEEDS CLIENT INPUT]` rather than invented. Score drops until the client supplies them.
- **S2 (42) — check 11, limitation:** aftercare content has fewer natural places for a limitation line. The "if it faded in three weeks, here's what that means" section partially covers it.
- **S3 (41) — check 14:** one paragraph in the "what actually goes wrong" section runs long by design (four linked failure modes). Flagged rather than hidden; will split if the client prefers.
- **S1 and S3 — check 20, local mentions:** comparison and objection articles are topic-led; forcing city mentions into them would read as stuffing. They carry one or zero local mentions deliberately. The local relevance load sits with the pillar, S4 and S5.

## D) Human pass — what I added that AI alone could not

1. **The 7-step process with real timings** — drawn from how the studio actually works, including why the sealing stage takes 40–60 minutes.
2. **Coastal humidity, hard water and beach proximity** as treatment variables — a national article cannot write these, and they're the reason the pillar passes the city-swap test.
3. **The refusal policy** ("if your hair can't take it, we say no") — repeated across four articles as the cluster's trust anchor.
4. **The "what a ₹2,000 keratin actually cuts" table** in S4 — the single most persuasive section in the cluster, and the one competitors can't publish without admitting their own shortcuts.
5. **Local price ranges** for MVP Colony services, positioned honestly against the studio's own rates.
6. **`[NEEDS CLIENT INPUT]` flags placed instead of invented numbers** — for product brands, post-treatment colour intervals, home-service radius and pregnancy policy.
7. **Cut 30% of the first drafts.** The drafts were 30–40% longer than the published versions, and the removed text was all explanation no one asked for.
