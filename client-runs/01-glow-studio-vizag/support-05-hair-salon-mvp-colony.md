## What to look for

MVP Colony has more salons per street than almost anywhere else in Visakhapatnam, so the choice is rarely about finding one — it's about finding one that will handle your hair honestly. Six things separate the ones worth your money from the ones that aren't.

**1. They ask about your hair history before recommending anything.**
What matters is what's been done to your hair in the last 6–12 months, not what it looks like today. A stylist who recommends keratin within five minutes of you sitting down hasn't assessed anything.

**2. They check your hair strand by strand.**
A proper assessment looks at porosity, elasticity and breakage. It takes 10–15 minutes and it should be free. If it isn't offered, ask for it.

**3. They publish or quote prices before the service.**
You should know the number before the first clip or the first bowl of colour. In this area, most salons quote a starting price and finalise it at the counter — which is where "₹3,000 at the door, ₹5,200 at the bill" stories come from.

**4. They do a patch test before colour.**
At least 24 hours before any colour service — including for regular clients. It's one extra trip and it's the difference between finding out about a reaction on a Tuesday or on your wedding morning.

**5. The senior stylist does the chemical work.**
Colour, keratin and smoothening are not jobs to learn on. Ask directly: "Will you be doing the service, or someone junior?" A studio that answers plainly is a studio worth using.

**6. They'll tell you when not to book.**
This is the rarest and the most useful. A salon that refuses your booking because your hair isn't in a fit state to be treated is protecting your hair at the cost of their own revenue. That's the single best signal available.

## Red flags

- Prices only quoted after the service
- No patch test before colour, "because you've been here before"
- Chemical services finished suspiciously fast (a full keratin cannot be done in an hour)
- The person who quotes you isn't the person who does the work, and nobody mentions this
- Pressure to add services while you're in the chair
- No written aftercare after a chemical treatment
- Reviews that are all five stars and all posted in one week

## Six questions to ask on the phone before you go

You can settle most of this in a two-minute call:

1. How much for [service] on hair of my length, and is that the final price?
2. Who will do the service — the senior stylist or someone else?
3. How long will it take, start to finish?
4. Do you do a patch test, and when?
5. Can you assess my hair before I commit — and is that free?
6. What aftercare do you provide afterwards?

A salon that answers these six comfortably deserves your appointment. One that sounds irritated by them has told you what the service will feel like.

## Services that need care in coastal Vizag

A few things behave differently here than in inland cities, and a salon in this area should know them:

- **Humidity and frizz.** Coastal humidity swells the cuticle. Treatments that work fine in dry climates need a proper seal here, or the frizz returns within weeks.
- **Hard water in some pockets.** MVP Colony and neighbouring areas vary in water hardness. Mineral buildup blocks colour and keratin from bonding, which is why a clarifying wash matters more here than in most places.
- **Beach and salt water.** Anyone swimming at Rushikonda or RK Beach should expect treatments to fade faster and rinse immediately after.
- **Wedding season pressure.** November to February is booked out. If your wedding is in that window, plan your trial 3–4 months ahead — bridal trials in this area fill fast.
- **Humidity + heat = styling fatigue.** Most people here over-wash and over-iron. A treatment that reduces both is usually a better investment than new products.
