## 2. Why this cluster and not another

**Selection criteria applied:**
1. **Revenue-connected.** Every article maps to a service the studio sells at ₹3,800–₹8,000. No traffic-for-traffic's-sake topics (no "hair care tips" listicles).
2. **Winnable.** Competitors publish thin service pages with no prices and no process detail. The cluster wins on depth and specificity, not on domain authority.
3. **Uncopyable.** The content leans on things only this studio can say: exact price ranges, the 24-hour patch test, the refusal policy, coastal-humidity experience, local landmarks.
4. **Cluster-shaped.** Each support answers a question that naturally precedes the pillar's decision, then hands the reader to it.
5. **Review-sustainable.** 6 articles over 3 months at the client's real capacity of 2/month. Not 20 articles nobody will fact-check.

**Rejected topics and why** (worth showing the client — it demonstrates strategy, not volume):

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| "10 hair care tips for monsoon" | No revenue link, competes with national beauty sites, zero conversion intent |
| "Best salon in Visakhapatnam" | Aggregators and directories dominate; unwinnable and unverifiable |
| "[Service] in every Vizag suburb" (10 location pages) | Template doorway pages — exactly what the March 2026 core update targeted |
| "Keratin treatment side effects" as a separate article | Overlaps Support 3's intent; would cannibalise. Covered as an H2 inside Support 3 instead |
| "Hair colour ideas 2026" | Traffic without buyers; wrong funnel stage for a service business |

## 3. Article briefs

### PILLAR — Keratin Treatment in Visakhapatnam: Cost, Process, Results & Honest Advice

- **Sections:** verdict table (who each is for) · one H2 per option · how a professional chooses · cost comparison · what happens if you pick wrong · FAQ · CTA

### Support 2 — How to Make a Keratin Treatment Last 5 Months Instead of 3

### Support 4 — Keratin Treatment Cost in Visakhapatnam

### Support 5 — How to Choose a Hair Salon in MVP Colony

## 6. What each article must contain (the standard, repeated)

1. **Answer in the first 90 words.**
2. **Real numbers** — prices, durations, counts — from the client's brief, each verified or flagged.
3. **One honest limitation** or "who this isn't for" section.
4. **One local, true detail** that a national site cannot write (humidity, water, landmarks, travel).
5. **A named author** with a credential relevant to the topic.
6. **One table** and **one list** minimum.
7. **FAQ block** matching real People-Also-Ask questions.
8. **2 internal links up** (supports) or **5 down** (pillar), with descriptive anchors.
9. **Author + published + updated dates.**
10. **A closing CTA** that says what to do and what it costs to ask.

Any article that can't meet all ten doesn't get published. That single rule is the difference between a cluster that compounds and one that gets classified as scaled content.
