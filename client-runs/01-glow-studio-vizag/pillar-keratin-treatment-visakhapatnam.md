## What a keratin treatment actually does

A keratin treatment coats the hair cuticle in a protein-based formula, then seals it with heat. It doesn't permanently change your hair's structure — your hair grows out naturally and the treatment fades gradually over months. What it does is smooth the outer layer, which is why frizz drops, drying time falls, and hair looks glossier with far less effort.

Two things matter more than the brand on the bottle:

1. **Whether your hair is in a fit state to receive the treatment.** Damaged, over-processed hair can't be sealed properly.
2. **Whether the stylist completes every step.** Keratin isn't hard. It's just long, and it's easy to rush.

Most people who say "keratin ruined my hair" went to a studio that skipped the clarifying wash, the section-by-section application, or the sealing — and usually all three.

## What actually happens: the process, step by step

You're in the chair for **3–4 hours** on average. Here's where that time goes.

1. **Strand assessment (15 minutes, free).** We check your hair section by section — porosity, previous chemical work, breakage. This is where we decide whether keratin is right for you at all.
2. **Clarifying wash (15 minutes).** A deep-cleansing wash removes product buildup, oil and residue. Skipping this is the single most common reason treatments fail early — the formula can't bond to coated hair.
3. **Application, section by section (60–90 minutes).** The formula goes on in small sections, saturating evenly. This is slow, repetitive work, and it's where a rushed studio cuts corners.
4. **Processing time (20–30 minutes).** The formula sits and works into the cuticle.
5. **Blow-dry (20–30 minutes).** Rough dry, then sectioned blow-dry.
6. **Sealing with heat (40–60 minutes).** Flat-ironed in thin sections at controlled temperature to seal the protein in. This step produces the smoothness; miss it and the result looks nothing like keratin.
7. **Finish and trim (15 minutes).** Ends tidied, and you leave with written aftercare instructions.

If a studio finishes a full keratin in under two hours, something above was compressed. That's the honest reason to compare process, not just price.

## How long it lasts (and what changes that)

**Typically 3–5 months.** Realistic ranges by situation:

| Your hair | Expected duration |
|---|---|
| Healthy, thick, well-maintained | 4–6 months |
| Fine or moderately damaged | 2–3 months |
| Heavily processed or bleached | 2 months — repair the hair first |
| Washing daily with regular shampoo | 6–8 weeks |
| Washing 2–3 times weekly, sulfate-free | Up to 5 months |

An **express or mini keratin** is a different, lighter service: it smooths frizz rather than restructuring, and it fades in **4–6 weeks**. It costs less and it isn't trying to last five months — worth knowing if a salon quotes you express pricing for a full keratin claim.

## Who this is NOT for

We turn bookings down for a reason, and it's the part most studios won't tell you.

- **Hair bleached twice or more in the last three months.** Keratin on hair that's already compromised can cause breakage. Do a repair treatment first — hair botox or a protein rebuild — then reassess in 6–8 weeks.
- **Anyone expecting permanent straightness.** It's not permanent and it's not a relaxer. Hair returns gradually to its natural texture.
- **Anyone wanting a 45-minute service.** There's no honest way to do a proper keratin in an hour.
- **Scalp conditions that are active or undiagnosed.** Get that looked at first; a patch test and a doctor's clearance come before any chemical service.
- **Anyone on a strict budget looking for the cheapest option.** A cheap treatment that fails in three weeks costs more than doing it once properly.

## Why coastal humidity changes the story in Visakhapatnam

Visakhapatnam isn't an inland city, and that matters for hair treatments. Three local factors affect how a keratin behaves here:

- **Humidity.** Coastal humidity swells the hair cuticle, which is why frizz returns faster here than in dry climates if the seal isn't completed properly. It also means healthy hair feels unmanageable by mid-morning in monsoon.
- **Water quality.** Harder water leaves mineral deposits that block the formula from bonding. That's why the clarifying wash matters more in some parts of the city than others, and it's one of the first things we check.
- **Heat and sun.** Beach days and chlorinated or salt water strip treatments quickly. If you swim, plan for the treatment to fade faster and book a follow-up earlier.

Anyone can write a keratin article. Only a studio working in this humidity can tell you that your treatment will behave differently in July than in December.

## What you have to do afterwards

The treatment decides how your hair starts. Your routine decides how long it lasts.

- **Don't wash for 48–72 hours.** Let the formula fully set.
- **Use sulfate-free shampoo.** Sulfates strip the coating — this is the biggest single factor in how long it lasts.
- **Wash 2–3 times a week**, not daily.
- **Use a silk pillowcase** if you can; cotton creates friction and creases.
- **Avoid chlorinated and salt water**, or rinse immediately after and use a protective leave-in.
- **Heat with care.** You'll need far less styling, so use that advantage.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does keratin cost in Visakhapatnam?**
Usually ₹4,500 to ₹8,000 at a professional studio, depending on length, thickness and product range. At Glow Studio it starts at ₹4,500, and the exact price is confirmed in writing after a free strand assessment. *(verify)*

**How long does a keratin treatment take?**
Three to four hours for most hair lengths, including wash, application, drying and sealing. Long or very thick hair can take longer.

**Will keratin damage my hair?**
Done correctly, no. Damage usually comes from skipping the clarifying wash or the sealing step, or from treating hair that's already over-processed. If your hair isn't suitable, we'll say so and suggest a repair plan instead. [Read the full answer →](/blog/does-keratin-damage-hair)

**Can I wash my hair after keratin?**
Not for 48–72 hours. After that, use sulfate-free shampoo and wash two to three times a week.

**How long does it last?**
Usually three to five months, depending on your hair type and how you wash it. Healthy hair with good aftercare sits at the top of that range.

**Is keratin treatment safe?**
Professional keratin is widely used and considered safe when applied correctly on suitable hair, with patch testing where required. Tell your stylist about any scalp condition, pregnancy or known sensitivities before booking, and ask what formula they use.

**Do you charge more for long hair?**
Yes — longer and thicker hair needs more product and more time. We confirm the difference before you book, not at the counter.

**What's the difference between keratin and smoothening?**
Keratin smooths and reduces frizz while keeping some volume and movement. Smoothening straightens flatter and longer. [Full comparison here →](/blog/keratin-vs-smoothening-vs-hair-botox)
