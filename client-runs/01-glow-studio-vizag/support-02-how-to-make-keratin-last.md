## The three things that matter most

How long a keratin treatment lasts depends far more on what you do after it than on the salon. Three habits decide most of it: **don't wash for 48–72 hours**, **switch to sulfate-free shampoo**, and **wash two to three times a week instead of daily**. Get those three right and you'll usually land at the top of the 3–5 month range instead of the bottom.

Everything below is the detail behind those three, plus the mistakes that quietly strip a treatment in six weeks.

---

## The first 72 hours

The formula is still setting. What you do in these three days affects the whole result:

- **No washing** for 48–72 hours.
- **No tying, clipping or tight ponytails** — clips leave kinks and creases that can become permanent marks in the treated hair.
- **Avoid helmets and tight hats** where possible.
- **No gym-sweat soaking**, no swimming.
- **Sleep with hair loose**, ideally on a silk or satin pillowcase.

If you must wash earlier (a wedding, an event), tell your stylist beforehand so they can plan the timing of your appointment around it. It's a scheduling problem, not a crisis — but it's easier to solve before than after.

## Washing: frequency, products and temperature

| Do | Don't | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Use **sulfate-free shampoo** | Sulfates (SLS, SLES) | Sulfates are detergents that strip the coating directly — the single biggest factor in early fading |
| Wash **2–3 times a week** | Daily washing | Every wash removes a little of the treatment |
| Use **warm, not hot** water | Scalding showers | Heat and prolonged wetting swell the cuticle |
| Apply **conditioner mid-length to ends** | Heavy conditioner at the roots | Keeps the roots from going flat |
| Rinse **thoroughly** | Leaving product in | Residue dulls the finish |

If you exercise daily, don't skip washing — just rinse with water or use a sulfate-free co-wash and shampoo less often. Sweat and salt sitting in treated hair is worse than a gentle wash.

**What to look for on a label:** "sulfate-free" and ideally "sodium chloride-free". Some clarifying and anti-dandruff shampoos are especially aggressive — keep those for after the treatment has fully faded.

## Heat, water, sun and swimming

- **Heat:** one of the benefits of keratin is that you need far less heat styling. Use that. When you do style, use a heat protectant and keep the iron below 180°C.
- **Sun:** UV dries the cuticle. If you're outdoors a lot, use a leave-in with UV protection or wear a scarf.
- **Chlorine and salt water:** both strip treatments quickly. Rinse immediately after swimming, and don't swim on consecutive days if you want the treatment to hold.
- **Hard water:** Visakhapatnam's water varies by area, and mineral-heavy water leaves deposits that dull the finish. A shower filter helps; so does a final rinse with filtered or bottled water if your area's supply is especially hard. Ask your stylist whether they noticed buildup during your clarifying wash — they'll know.
- **Humidity:** coastal humidity is unavoidable here, but a sealed cuticle handles it far better than an untreated one. Don't skip the sealing-stage maintenance just because the first month feels effortless.

## Monthly maintenance between treatments

- **A hair spa every 4–6 weeks** tops up moisture without interfering with the treatment. It's the cheapest way to extend the result.
- **Trim the ends every 8–10 weeks.** Split ends travel upward and make treated hair look tired faster.
- **Deep condition weekly** if your hair is dry or you swim.
- **Brush gently from the ends up**, with a wide-tooth comb on wet hair.
- **Rotate your pillowcase** — silk or satin, washed regularly.

## Signs it's fading early (and what to do)

| What you notice | Likely cause | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| Frizz returns around week 4–6 | Washed daily, or used a sulfate shampoo | Switch products, reduce washing, book a hair spa |
| Ends feel dry and rough but roots are smooth | Normal wear at the oldest part of the hair | Trim the ends; it's not a failed treatment |
| Hair feels coated and stiff | Clarifying wash was skipped at the salon | Ask the studio to assess — a clarifying treatment can help |
| Patchy texture, some sections smooth and others not | Uneven application or uneven sealing | Requires assessment; don't treat over it |
| Everything faded within 3 weeks | Treatment type mismatch (express sold as full) or product issue | Discuss with the studio; a reputable one will want to know |

**The honest note:** if your treatment faded in three weeks, go back and tell the salon. Good studios want that information — and it's usually fixable. What you shouldn't do is book another keratin immediately to cover it.

## A simple weekly routine

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Days 1–3 after treatment** | No washing, no tying, no swimming |
| **First wash** | Sulfate-free shampoo, warm water, air-dry where possible |
| **Every wash after** | Sulfate-free, conditioner mid-length to ends, pat dry |
| **Weekly** | Deep condition or leave-in mask if hair feels dry |
| **Every 4–6 weeks** | Hair spa at the salon |
| **Every 8–10 weeks** | Trim the ends |
| **Daily** | Loose styles, minimal heat, protect from sun if outdoors |
| **As needed** | Rinse immediately after swimming |

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does keratin last if I wash my hair every day?**
Expect 6–8 weeks instead of 3–5 months. Daily washing with a regular sulfate shampoo is the fastest way to strip a treatment.

**Can I use my normal shampoo after keratin?**
Only if it's sulfate-free. Check the label for SLS and SLES, and switch back only after the treatment has fully faded.

**Can I tie my hair after keratin?**
Not for the first 48–72 hours. After that, yes — but use soft ties and avoid tight styles that leave creases.

**Does keratin wash out completely?**
It fades gradually rather than washing out. You'll notice the smoothness reducing over the last month, and your natural texture returning progressively.

**Will a hair spa shorten or extend my keratin?**
A moisture-focused hair spa extends it, when done with appropriate products. Avoid aggressive clarifying or detox treatments during the treatment's life.

**What's the best shampoo to use in Visakhapatnam's water?**
Sulfate-free, sodium-chloride-free, and ideally paired with a shower filter if your area's water is hard. Ask your stylist what they noticed about buildup during your clarifying wash.
